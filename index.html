Auto Click

Yana danna maɓallai a madadinka, a cikin apps ɗin da ka zaɓa.

Sauke Auto Click

Version 1.1, Android 5.0 zuwa sama, kyauta

Yadda yake aiki

Ka zaɓi app, sannan ka rubuta kalmar da ke jikin maɓalli, misali Continue. Duk lokacin da wannan kalma ta fito a allon app ɗin, Auto Click yana danna ta da kansa bayan jiran lokacin da ka tsara. Kowace kalma tana aiki da kanta, kuma kana iya ƙara, gyara ko cire ta a kowane lokaci.

Misali na rules a cikin app

Continuejira: 500 ms
Allowjira: 300 ms
Skipjira: 800 ms

Yadda ake shigarwa

  1. Ka sauke fayil ɗin APK da maɓallin da ke sama.
  2. Ka buɗe fayil ɗin. Idan wayarka ta tambaya, ka ba browser ɗinka izinin Install unknown apps, sannan ka danna Install.
  3. Ka buɗe Auto Click, ka danna Kunna izinin Accessibility, sannan ka kunna Auto Click. Idan ya ce Restricted setting, ka je Settings, Apps, Auto Click, digo uku a sama, ka zaɓi Allow restricted settings, sannan ka dawo ka kunna.
  4. Ka ƙara app da kalmomin da kake so a danna, sannan ka kunna ON.

Game da izinin Accessibility

Auto Click yana buƙatar izinin Accessibility domin ya ga kalmomin da ke kan allo ya kuma danna maɓallin. Yana aiki ne kawai a apps da ka zaɓa da kanka.

Auto Click ba shi da izinin intanet kwata-kwata, don haka ba zai iya aika komai daga wayarka ba.

Google Play Protect na iya nuna gargaɗi game da apps masu Accessibility. Wannan na yau da kullum ne. Wasu apps na banki da biyan kuɗi suna toshe irin wannan app, don haka ba za su yi aiki tare da shi ba.
